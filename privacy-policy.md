## 1. Information Collection and Use

Our applications are designed to be lightweight, user-friendly, and privacy-respecting.

### Personal Information
We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII)—such as your full name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts list, or precise real-time location. You do not need to register an account or provide personal details to use our applications.

### Local Device Storage
All user-specific application data (such as memorization progress, bookmarks, quiz scores, reading history, saved preferences, and notification schedules) is stored **locally on your device** using Android DataStore, SharedPreferences, or local SQLite storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to our servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To support monetization, application stability, and continuous improvement, our applications integrate trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google and Firebase. These third-party services may collect certain device-level information (such as IP address, device model, operating system version, and Google Advertising ID) in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads Consent (UMP SDK):**  
  Used to display advertisements and manage user consent in compliance with privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA). Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers and cookies/local storage to serve ads.  
  - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)  
  - [Google AdMob Privacy and Terms](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

- **Google Analytics for Firebase:**  
  Used to collect aggregated, anonymous statistics regarding app interactions (e.g., screen views, feature usage) to help us understand and improve user experience.  
  - [Google Analytics for Firebase Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics)

- **Firebase Crashlytics:**  
  Used to gather anonymous stack traces and crash logs when an unexpected error occurs, allowing us to identify and resolve stability issues quickly.  
  - [Firebase Crashlytics Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

- **Firebase Remote Config:**  
  Used to dynamically configure app parameters and feature flags without requiring an immediate app update.  
  - [Firebase Remote Config Terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):**  
  Used where applicable to deliver optional notifications and relevant informational updates to your device.  
  - [Firebase Cloud Messaging Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 3. Device Permissions

Depending on the specific application and features you use, our applications may request the following permissions:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to communicate with third-party services (AdMob ads, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config).
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to verify active internet connectivity before making network requests.
- `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: On Android 13 (API level 33) and above, used to deliver optional local study reminders or notifications if enabled by the user.
- `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 12+ to access the Advertising Identifier for ad personalization or frequency capping (subject to user consent).

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not operate external user account databases or collect personal data on dedicated servers, we do not retain your personal data. 

- **Local Data Deletion:** You can delete all your stored progress and preferences at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device settings (`Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Storage > Clear Data`) or by uninstalling the application.
- **Advertising Data:** For data processed by Google services (such as advertising IDs), you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads directly within your Android device settings (`Settings > Google > Ads`).
