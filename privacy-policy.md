## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Asmaul Husna: Memory Practice** is designed to provide a privacy-first educational experience for learning and memorizing the 99 Names of Allah (Asmaul Husna). 

### Personal Information
We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII)—such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location. You do not need to register an account or provide any personal details to use the application.

### Local Device Storage
All your practice data, including memorization progress, test scores, bookmarks, audio/text preferences, and reminder settings, is stored **locally on your device** using Android DataStore / local storage. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

## 3. Device Permissions

The application requests only minimal permissions necessary for its intended operation:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to communicate with third-party services (AdMob ads, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config).
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to check network connectivity before attempting to fetch online configurations or ads.
- `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: On Android 13 (API level 33) and above, this optional permission allows the app to send local daily practice reminders scheduled by the user. Notifications are scheduled locally on the device; no notification data is sent across the internet.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not operate external user account databases or collect personal data on dedicated servers, we do not retain your personal data. 

- **Local Data Deletion:** You can delete all your stored progress and preferences at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device settings (`Settings > Apps > Asmaul Husna: Memory Practice > Storage > Clear Data`) or by uninstalling the application.
- **Third-Party Data:** For data processed by Google services (such as advertising IDs), you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads directly within your Android device settings (`Settings > Google > Ads`).
